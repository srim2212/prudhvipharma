About Prudhvi Pharma

Three decades of
trust & excellence

Prudhvi Pharmaceuticals was founded in 1995 by Mr. Venkata Rama Prasad Sadhu in Vijayawada, Krishna District (now NTR District). What began as a single distribution firm has grown into a multi-vertical pharma supply chain enterprise trusted by 150+ pharmaceutical companies across India.

Today the group spans distribution, C&F agency, super stockist, and proprietary company operations — all built on the founder's values of dedication, hard work, and commitment.
